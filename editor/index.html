Tiles

Platform path

Pick the Path tool (Y), paint a platform, then click it.

Path tool: click a platform or saw to select it, click tiles to add stops (max 8), right-click removes the last stop. Erasing the tile deletes its route. Playtest to watch it ride.

Effect zone

Zone tool (X): drag a rect to paint the zone. Right-click a zone to delete it. Wind power 1–3.

Level text

No collision. Click to stamp. Erase, right-click, or Delete removes a label — click the letters, not just the center tile.

Level images

PNG or JPG. Big files get compressed to base64. Click an image to move it; drag the 8 handles to resize. Delete removes the selected one. Up to 8 per level.

Level HTML

Embed styled text or boxes. Click to move it; drag the 8 handles to resize. Right-click it to pick a layer (In front = clickable links), edit, or delete. Double-click edits the code. Up to 8 per level.

Spike rotation

Mouse wheel over canvas rotates spikes, invis spikes, and fake spikes. [ ] also work. Invisible tiles show as faded + INV and vanish in playtest. Fake bricks and spikes look real in play but have no collision.

Offset

Shift objects off the grid by up to 24 pixels. New tiles use this offset. Select tiles and press Shift+arrows to nudge them (hold Alt for 4px).

Prefabs

Select tiles, then press K (or the button) to keep them as a prefab for other levels. Click a prefab to stamp it. Right-click a saved one to delete it. Hold Alt to play from the hovered cell.
